# Rumors, Witnesses and Information Propagation

**Outcome:** Information spreads through plausible people, media and institutions rather than magical universal awareness.

## Scope
- Represent source, recipient, channel, delay, distortion and confidence for noteworthy communications.
- Bound gossip/rumor propagation by time, proximity, affiliations and attention.
- Allow lies and mistakes without modifying the authoritative facts they describe.

## Milestones
1. Transmit one true rumor across two actors with a delay.
2. Introduce a false retelling and trace who believes it.
3. Let factions/public media spread news at scale without per-person LLM agents.

## Proving scenario
A distant rival hears a misleading account weeks later and acts on that belief; the actual insult and witnesses remain unchanged.

## Independence and interfaces
Uses the minimal evidence/knowledge format when provided; small authored communications work independently without simulating a world network.

## Open questions
- What is the cheapest propagation model?
- When do rumor variants earn separate identities?
- Who can see the propagation trace?
