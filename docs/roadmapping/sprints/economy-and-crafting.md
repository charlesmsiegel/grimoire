# Trade, Crafting and Resource Economies

**Outcome:** Make buying, selling, consuming supplies and producing goods coherent without forcing rigid economics on story-first campaigns.

## Scope
- Represent currency and units, pricing/availability and multi-party trade as explicit proposals and effects.
- Support craft recipes, costs, work-in-progress and rewards where the chosen game supports them.
- Keep shortage, acquisition and scarcity meaningful while allowing manually narrated transactions.

## Milestones
1. Trade an item for two resources with a visible balance check.
2. Craft a multi-component item and charge ingredients once.
3. Add optional regional markets, supply, labor and skill-linked production.

## Moonshot
A living, historically plausible trade economy responds to wars, travel, monopolies and player actions without requiring simulation of every merchant.

## Proving scenario
A second submission of the same purchase never duplicates money or item; an unowned ingredient causes a visible refusal.

## Independence and interfaces
Layer on inventory, time and mechanics capabilities when present; the initial transaction can use simple counted currency.

## Open questions
- Which units need exact precision?
- When should price be authored versus inferred?
- How is an open-ended barter agreement committed?
