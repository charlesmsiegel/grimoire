# Scene and Scenario Preparation

**Outcome:** Help a GM set up compelling playable situations from world facts without forcing players down the prepared route.

## Scope
- Suggest scene premises, cast, location, open questions, clues, tensions and meaningful starting stakes.
- Ground proposals in current secrets, NPC goals, calendar and player choices.
- Keep suggestions non-canonical until accepted and avoid gratuitous preparation inference.

## Milestones
1. Prepare one scene with credible stakes and two possible directions.
2. Offer a linked set of optional scenarios without imposing a sequence.
3. Design a flexible multi-session scenario kit with reusable notes and consequences.

## Moonshot
A creative co-director continually finds powerful dramatic opportunities while respecting emerging stories and player refusal.

## Proving scenario
A proposed meeting scene adapts when the player decides to leave town; no unplayed premise becomes a recorded fact.

## Independence and interfaces
Can live inside the existing new-scene chooser; narrative arcs, full world simulation and agents may enhance but are not necessary.

## Open questions
- Which prep details should be structured?
- When are suggestions stale?
- How can the user reject a proposed scene without repeated insistence?
