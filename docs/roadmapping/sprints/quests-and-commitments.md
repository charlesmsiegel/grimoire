# Quests, Deadlines and Persistent Commitments

**Outcome:** Keep obligations and unresolved goals intelligible as play diverges from any intended plot.

## Scope
- Represent objectives, stages, deadlines, promises, dependencies and abandoned goals without forcing a progress bar.
- Connect opportunities and consequences to existing chronicle/ledger facts and clock updates.
- Preserve commitments through scene transfer, retcon and conflicting narrative evidence.

## Milestones
1. Track a promise and deadline across several scenes.
2. Record fulfillment, abandonment and partial completion with provenance.
3. Support branching goals and optional quest planning aids.

## Proving scenario
The party refuses the assigned mission but the world still records the refusal and deadline; later scenes do not claim it was completed.

## Independence and interfaces
Built on existing ledger and timeline authority; does not need formal mechanics, world map or narrative arc planner.

## Open questions
- When is a commitment structured versus prose?
- How should contradictions and uncertain completion be resolved?
- What is the minimum deadline schema?
