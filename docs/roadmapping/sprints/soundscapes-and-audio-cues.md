# Scene Soundscapes and Diegetic Audio

**Outcome:** Enhance a scene with optional background ambience and precise sound cues without affecting factual story authority.

## Scope
- Allow scene/location-linked ambience, music, effects and audio ducking under speech.
- Use explicit, deterministic or reviewed cues without new inference on every text turn.
- Respect accessibility, mute, licensing, looping and independent volume settings.

## Milestones
1. Play an optional scene atmosphere that follows the view.
2. Cue a sound effect from existing scene metadata and stop it on cancellation.
3. Support spatial audio layers and author-directed dynamic soundscapes.

## Moonshot
A responsive sound designer builds immersive acoustic environments and character placement without treating generated noises as canonical events.

## Proving scenario
A thunder effect can be muted or disabled; it cannot make NPCs react to a storm unless the scene actually records one.

## Independence and interfaces
Independent media renderer; may integrate VN or live voice later, but text-first RP is unchanged.

## Open questions
- Which sounds are ambience versus actual events?
- How should ducking, looping and latency behave?
- Which content/licensing sources are permitted?
