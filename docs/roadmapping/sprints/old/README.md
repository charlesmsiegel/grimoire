These are the **26 prior planning proposals**, moved from `docs/roadmapping/sprints/` to `docs/roadmapping/sprints/old/`. Their original substantive text and open questions remain preserved. One relative link to Principles in `state-transactions.md` was rebased to keep it working from this folder; Git history retains the exact originals.

Their broader scopes remain valuable historical context, but the [new roadmap](../../README.md) and [capability lookup](../../capabilities.md) are the current planning authority. The archived files' links to one another continue to point to archived versions, not the new scoped proposals.

They are **not** additional active sprints or approved implementation plans, and they do not imply previously delivered foundations should be rebuilt.
