# Narrative Arcs, Scenarios and Consequences

**Priority:** after usable action/continuity integration; independent story-planning helpers may be staged sooner.

## Goal

Support satisfying stories emerging from player actions, NPC agendas and ongoing conditions without forcing predetermined plots, omniscient cast knowledge or narratorial overrides of known mechanical outcomes.

## Work to plan

- **Narrative Arcs:** track developing themes, pressures, dilemmas and unresolved situations across scenes. Arcs guide emphasis, not a secret requirement that a chosen plot endpoint must happen.
- **Scenario Design:** help author/open suggested premises, locations, cast, tensions and available hooks, with optional bounded scenario preparation rather than unconditional extra inference.
- **Consequences:** maintain state changes and durable follow-up from social, exploratory and mechanical outcomes, including successes, partials, failures, delays and meaningful refusals.
- **Continuity and knowledge:** respect character perspective, audience, past scenes, diary/chronicle, timeline, established decisions, relationships and later retcons.
- **Quests and obligations:** support objectives, threads, deadlines, fulfillment, abandonment and obligations without mapping every human motive into a progress bar.
- **Emergent storytelling:** reactions can create new situations and multiple possible resolutions. The player may pursue unexpected alternatives; storyteller pacing/difficulty policy can alter opportunities but never retrospectively rewrite a die result.
- **Long-form structure:** help reorganize and navigate chapters/scene groupings, keep export-worthy prose/POV metadata and distinguish narrative recap from canonical state.

## Proving scenario

The group loses an important negotiation. The authoritative relation and faction objectives change, the player can attempt a different route, and the next suggested scene reflects the altered facts. The story arc remains meaningful without the narrator undoing the loss or hallucinating an unearned ally.

## Dependencies and boundaries

[Challenges](challenges.md) provides obstacles and opportunities, [Societies](societies.md) supplies motivations, [World Simulation](world-simulation.md) supplies offscreen development, [State Transactions](state-transactions.md) commits factual consequences, [Campaign Tuning](campaign-tuning.md) controls emphasis, and [Context & Evaluation](context-evaluation.md) checks consistency.

## Open questions

- How much of an arc, scenario or quest should be durable structured data rather than a concise prose concept?
- How are narrative goals kept from overriding player agency and causal/rules authority?
- Which details are known publicly, privately, to the director only, or unknown in a future scene?
- When should new scenario suggestions consume inference and how are irrelevant suggestions filtered?
- Which consequences are immediate effects, queued clocks, remembered summaries or future hooks?
