# Character Voices, Casting and Expressive Speech

**Outcome:** Give individually recognizable optional voices to narrator and characters with faithful attribution and readable transcripts.

## Scope
- Bind a voice profile to character/source/version or narrator; preserve overrides, license/consent and missing-voice fallback.
- Generate synchronized speech for already-authorized dialogue, with timings and optional mood/prosody cues.
- Keep canonical text independent from TTS pronunciation, performance or generated speech edits.

## Milestones
1. Assign separate voices to two NPCs and a narrator in transcript playback.
2. Support author-controlled pronunciation, prosody and expression without modifying text.
3. Render dramatic multi-character speech with inspectable cue choices.

## Moonshot
Performances express subtle subtext, emotion, pacing and long-term character development with the quality and consistency of an audio drama cast.

## Proving scenario
A scene read aloud retains correct voices after an outfit/version switch, works as text when audio is disabled, and exposes the source of a disputed voice choice.

## Independence and interfaces
Standalone TTS output over existing transcript; live input and conversational sessions are optional consumers.

## Open questions
- Are voice profiles identity-specific or portrayal-specific?
- How are third-party voice rights and consent managed?
- Who controls emotional inflection versus narrator intent?
