# Story Graph, Timeline and Continuity Explorer

**Outcome:** Let users understand long stories, follow character threads and navigate their consequences rather than hunting through thousands of posts.

## Scope
- Improve scene/story graph, timelines, cast histories, plot threads, event search and navigable evidence.
- Distinguish confirmed events, character beliefs and mere proposals visually.
- Provide branch comparisons, bookmark/annotation and fast history navigation.

## Milestones
1. Find every scene involving one character and one commitment.
2. Show a branch difference and a contradictory claim with source links.
3. Offer an interactive timeline of long-term causal and relationship changes.

## Moonshot
A living literary atlas visualizes the entire campaign as connected character arcs, conflicts and revelations without confusing interpretation with truth.

## Proving scenario
A player tracks an early promise through ten later scenes and sees when it was fulfilled or contradicted.

## Independence and interfaces
Read-only first using existing Ledger/Timeline/Story Graph; does not need deep world-simulation or new state writes.

## Open questions
- What graph views are actually navigable at scale?
- Which associations are explicit vs inferred?
- How is spoiler exposure controlled?
