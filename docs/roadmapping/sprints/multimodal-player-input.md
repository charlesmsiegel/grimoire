# Multimodal Player Input and Creative Referencing

**Outcome:** A player can indicate fictional intent using text, images, marked-up maps, sketches and eventually gesture without forcing a voice or image-generating interface.

## Scope and ownership
- Stage imported images, sketches, diagrams or annotated maps as reviewable **player evidence or intent**, not automatically canonical world facts.
- Support selection and reference to visual regions, object/actor disambiguation and explicit approval of inferred meaning.
- Associate source/rights/privacy and corrections with an accepted interpretation; prohibit silent mutation of established scenes.
- Keep typed input as a full alternative and expose model/provider limits honestly.

## Milestones
1. Let a player attach and annotate a scene reference to clarify an action or appearance.
2. Extract proposed positions/objects from an annotated room or map, requiring confirmation before writing.
3. Explore gesture, screen-pointing and live multimodal participation with appropriate access controls.

## Moonshot
A player naturally points, draws or shows a prop while playing; Grimoire understands the intended fictional reference and responds consistently through text, voice or an immersive scene.

## Proving scenario
A user circles a doorway on an imported sketch and says 'I hide here'; Grimoire offers the identified spot as a draft action rather than silently changing the world map.

## Interfaces and independence
Input staging can run with existing attachment handling; [Scene Physical Continuity](scene-physical-continuity.md), map tools and [Voice Routing](spoken-command-and-intent.md) are optional downstream consumers.
