# Factions, Institutions and Collective Decisions

**Outcome:** Groups have goals and internal disagreements rather than acting as a single omniscient person.

## Scope
- Track leadership, membership, factions within factions, resources, territory and policy.
- Model collective choices and disagreement separately from individual member preferences.
- Apply outcomes over time and propagate knowledge only through credible channels.

## Milestones
1. Create two groups with conflicting goals and explicit memberships.
2. Resolve one internal disagreement and a public decision.
3. Advance strategic plans through offscreen events with bounded effort.

## Moonshot
Emergent institutional politics, negotiations, shifting coalitions and diplomacy remain plausible across decades of player-driven history.

## Proving scenario
A guild leader rejects a deal while one guild member secretly assists the PC; the distant chapter does not instantly learn either choice.

## Independence and interfaces
Builds on group records already in Grimoire; can work without a full economy or general agent runtime.

## Open questions
- Who can commit collective choices?
- How do leadership changes affect standing goals?
- What abstract resources are necessary?
