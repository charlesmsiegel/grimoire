# Complete Combat Encounter and Tactical Turn Orchestration

**Outcome:** Play a coherent multi-round encounter rather than a collection of isolated dice rolls, with mechanics-specific turns, actors and stakes.

## Scope and ownership
- Use the selected ruleset's initiative/turn, action economy, reactions, timing, conditions and legal choices rather than enforcing d20 assumptions.
- Track participants, positions/targets when relevant, resources, pending choices and committed results across an entire encounter.
- Support player-declared tactics, unusual solutions, retreat, surrender, reinforcements, environmental interactions and narrative stakes.
- Provide clear progress, undo/redo boundaries, interruption and recovery; the narration must match resolved actions.

## Milestones
1. Orchestrate a three-actor combat over two rounds with unambiguous actions and committed effects under one supported module.
2. Include status, movement/cover and interrupts where the module supports them.
3. Support several radically different combat/contest structures, including theater-of-the-mind and supernatural systems.

## Moonshot
Grimoire is a tactically competent, narratively compelling GM capable of adjudicating cinematic or crunchy encounters as well as a dedicated human referee.

## Proving scenario
A prone combatant cannot illegally act as if standing under an applicable ruleset; an unexpected diplomatic surrender ends combat without requiring the planned final attack.

## Interfaces and independence
Consumes [Mechanical Resolution](mechanical-resolution.md) and accepted [Action Recovery](durable-action-recovery.md). Local positioning is optional where a system does not require it; this owns encounter sequence, **not** mechanics implementations.

## Open questions
- Which game systems need rounds vs exchanges vs narrative contests?
- How is player control preserved during reactions and interrupts?
- How do combat edits affect committed rolls?
