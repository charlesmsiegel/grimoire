high play_gate_extremes

The live play regression gate only catches an all-pass to all-fail reversal

For one fixture/check, off results [pass, pass, pass] and on results [fail, fail, pass] do not produce a regression: fails_on requires every on repeat to fail. The criterion can therefore miss a large degradation even when off is consistently sound. This follows the documented hard criterion; the concern is what that criterion establishes, not an implementation mismatch. The available narrative reports only three repeats per fixture/configuration and concurrent features.

Blast radius: Using gate.no_turn_regression as evidence for exposing or defaulting play switches.
