# Obstacles, Encounters and Open-Ended Solutions

**Outcome:** Present problems with stakes and multiple credible responses rather than encounters engineered toward one solution.

## Scope
- Describe goals, constraints, actors, clues, resources, stakes, escalation and failure modes.
- Support negotiation, stealth, preparation, violence, bypassing or declining an encounter.
- Apply the world's facts and selected mechanics where relevant; never force a predetermined result.

## Milestones
1. Author one guarded-archive obstacle with several viable approaches.
2. Allow a novel player approach and meaningful partial/failure consequences.
3. Offer optional procedural encounter design grounded in actor motives and setting.

## Proving scenario
A player bribes a guard instead of fighting; the choice affects reputation/resources and remains valid without a combat engine.

## Independence and interfaces
Can create playable situations using freeform narration and current state; quests, maps and mechanics enrich but do not block it.

## Open questions
- How structured must the initial obstacle be?
- How do we detect railroading in evaluation?
- What is the appropriate source of hidden stakes?
