# Downtime, Travel and Extended Activities

**Outcome:** Resolve weeks of study, recovery, research or travel as credible player decisions instead of an unexplained time jump.

## Scope
- Represent a bounded intent, duration, expected interruptions, progress and resources.
- Use selected mechanics for seasonal studies or crafting when available; support purely fictional outcomes otherwise.
- Offer compressed narration or played-out interim scenes with reviewable effects.

## Milestones
1. Resolve one 20-day study activity and record meaningful progress.
2. Integrate optional food, deadlines and partial outcomes.
3. Support overlapping commitments, pauses, interruption and Ars Magica season work.

## Proving scenario
Researching a language during travel advances the calendar once, updates progress, checks supplies if tracked and does not duplicate results on retry.

## Independence and interfaces
Consumes generic time advancement and accepted events; initially can use a simplified calendar adapter and need not simulate every NPC.

## Open questions
- One long intent or a queue of staged activities?
- When should play interrupt a time skip?
- Which intermediate results require player review?
