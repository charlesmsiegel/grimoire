# Narrative Arcs, Themes and Emergent Consequences

**Outcome:** Long-form stories gain shape and payoff without converting player choices into a fixed screenplay.

## Scope
- Track thematic tensions, unresolved threads, setups, reversals and possible payoffs as guidance rather than facts.
- Let choices redirect emphasis, produce new pressures and leave threads incomplete.
- Keep narrative planning downstream of authoritative actions, time and NPC beliefs.

## Milestones
1. Expose one optional arc and its current open tensions.
2. Replan after an unexpected failure without fabricating a success.
3. Offer multi-arc pacing and long-horizon callbacks with provenance.

## Moonshot
A director reasons about literary structure and character trajectories across thousands of scenes while never secretly changing player-established outcomes.

## Proving scenario
The party loses a negotiation; the planned alliance does not materialize, but the arc finds a meaningful new direction.

## Independence and interfaces
May begin as read-only story-planning over current ledger/story graph; obstacle planning is a separate feature.

## Open questions
- What parts of an arc should be structured?
- When should an arc be retired?
- How are writer/GM notes kept from NPC context?
