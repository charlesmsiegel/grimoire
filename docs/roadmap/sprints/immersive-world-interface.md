# Immersive Places, Avatars and Spatial Presence

**Outcome:** Offer optional spatial embodiment of stories for players who want more than a transcript or VN stage.

## Scope
- Represent a scene's places, actors, orientation and player focus as presentation metadata distinct from story canon.
- Support desktop 2D/3D, accessible alternatives and optional VR/AR where justified.
- Avoid invented actor actions or hidden movement from non-authoritative animation.

## Milestones
1. Prototype an explorable single-room scene using current character/location records.
2. Synchronize embodied presence with textual cast, location and explicit movement.
3. Explore optional VR/AR and interactive spatial performance with author-controlled fidelity.

## Moonshot
A locally controllable holodeck-like scene expresses a living RP world in spatial audio, responsive avatars and natural movement without surrendering player agency.

## Proving scenario
Walking an avatar across a rendered room does not teleport canonical character state until an explicit movement event is accepted.

## Independence and interfaces
Entirely optional view; a static-location prototype can run independently of full maps, audio or multiplayer.

## Open questions
- Which avatar/movement interactions should become canonical?
- What hardware/performance budgets are realistic?
- How do we prevent presentation from fabricating events?
