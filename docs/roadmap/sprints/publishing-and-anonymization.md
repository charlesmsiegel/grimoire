# Campaign Books, Exports and Privacy Filters

**Outcome:** Turn selected campaign scenes into polished readable books without altering canonical names, private metadata or history.

## Scope
- Export Markdown, HTML, plain text and EPUB from a structured snapshot.
- Support formal OOC/POV/chapter metadata, scene grouping and stable per-export aliases.
- Preview removal/redaction of mechanics, author labels, private facts and spoilers with no brittle prose rewrites.

## Milestones
1. Produce a readable EPUB and Markdown export from one branch.
2. Apply metadata-based exclusions and character aliases reproducibly.
3. Offer layout/illustration themes, chapter editing and advanced POV exports.

## Moonshot
A publication studio transforms a campaign into a coherent, edited novel with traceable editorial suggestions and human approval, not hidden changes to play history.

## Proving scenario
An EPUB hides chosen OOC sections and anonymizes a player name while canonical campaign files remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

## Independence and interfaces
Works from current scenes and export infrastructure; world packages for sharing records are a separate capability.

## Open questions
- What metadata is needed to filter reliably?
- Which redactions need human review?
- Should export history be canonical or a local derived artifact log?
