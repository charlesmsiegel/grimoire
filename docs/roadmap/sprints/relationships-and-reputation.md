# Personal Relationships, Trust and Reputation

**Outcome:** People remember significant interactions and form believable, asymmetric attitudes toward each other.

## Scope
- Track debts, trust, fear, rivalry, affection, obligations and situated changes with provenance.
- Keep private beliefs and public reputations distinct rather than one global attitude meter.
- Expose narrative context and optional numeric interpretations without forcing scores on every relationship.

## Milestones
1. Record a meaningful interpersonal interaction and its consequences.
2. Model opposing perspectives and a change of allegiance.
3. Link public reputation to particular communities while retaining uncertainty.

## Proving scenario
A guild envoy personally resents an insult, but absent members do not all immediately despise the player.

## Independence and interfaces
Can build on existing relationship/ledger records; rumor transport and autonomous choices are separate users.

## Open questions
- Which traits deserve first-class structure?
- How should relationship strength be represented?
- When can the narrator propose an attitude change?
