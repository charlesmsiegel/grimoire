The pure group_refs leaf parses their lenient effective values and preserves raw
legacy tokens on edits. Overlay owns the difference between inherited World
slots and campaign-authored slots: a detached campaign actor cannot silently
become the World actor reached by the same spelling. A first copy reviews exact
unresolved retained slots; publication proves destination identities again.

The file is the authority. API/context Pydantic models report a same-read view;
manual Members edits journal a compare-and-swap against the owned original.
Relocation suspends that original's offers before freeing its slug, deletion
retires them, and Undo cannot substitute a later occupant or World fallback.
S5 can extend these fences to reviewed batch positions without creating another
roster authority.

Filesystem aliases are evidence within a namespace. Campaign Off/base keys
identify owned originals; World Gone/asset keys identify World originals.
RecordIdentityRefs freezes these two proven sets before paths disappear. They
cannot be unioned: one root can hold distinct case-sensitive files while the
other merges their spellings. A transfer compares canonical IDs within each
root, using native samefile proofs; it never compares copy and ancestor inodes.
Ambiguous adoption refuses before effects. Whole strict ledgers are read under
owning campaign holds, and current World bindings select the held dependents.

The cost is conservative refusal when identity or access cannot be proven.
Saved appearance keys under another alias require repair before promotion.

S5 campaign-discovery barrier. The known asset-alias successor display minor is
recorded in the execution ledger; no frozen campaign home was regenerated.
