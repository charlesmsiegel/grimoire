# Roadmap 09-S6: synthetic long-history evaluation and final verification

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Measure retrieval on synthetic short and long campaigns, wire offline/live CLI arms, and prove every hard eligibility and budget rule.
**Architecture:** A seeded generator writes actual store records to a temporary library; gold identities and post ranges are independent of retrieval. Recorded synthetic vectors make semantic arms deterministic offline. Live calls use existing eval scopes and reporting.
**Tech Stack:** Python eval harness, real store APIs, existing eval ledger/report comparison machinery.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-roadmap-09-hybrid-historical-retrieval-design.md`, 09-S6 and section 12.

## Global Constraints

- Synthetic data only: Seraphine, Mara, Winifred, Realm, Saltmarch and role labels. Never read or describe a real library. Generate long campaigns, do not check in store trees.
- Cases 1–10 cover old evidence, wording distractor, structure without words, generic query, short excerpt in long transcript, closed branch, future scene, exclusions/secrecy, first/young campaigns, and identity-less scenes.
- Arms: context-only, lexical, semantic, structural+lexical, hybrid; hybrid+rerank live. Offline semantic/hybrid replay recorded synthetic vectors.
- Metrics: recall at 1/3/5, excerpt recall, hard rules, added packed tokens, file/network/wall time, embed rows by task, decide calls, per-arm tokens and three separate money columns.
- `evals/run.py --history`, `--history --long`, and `--history --live`; ordinary offline corpus runs inside pytest. No live provider call in tests or ordinary verification.
- Default remains off. Gate: hybrid recall@3 at least every other arm, strictly above context-only/lexical, no hard-rule failure and added tokens within ceiling.

## Review Focus

- Gold scene and excerpt identities must come from planted facts, not the retrieval outputs.
- A semantic replay arm must exercise the production scorer and space/text cache keys.
- Long recipes must test the 64/1000 bounds and shared-group widening counterexample.
- Live mode must meter embedding/rerank/downstream generation under the eval scope without mixing money columns.
- Non-turn tier 3 must not contaminate a normal turn or collapse equal legacy sid keys across campaigns; document and test the caller-directed source scope contract.
