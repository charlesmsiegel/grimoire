# Roadmap 09-S3: the narrator turn retrieval phase

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Gather structural/lexical history outside locks and pass it into each ordinary narrator compose.
**Architecture:** The async route helper offloads disk/CPU work to workers; def routes bridge through the lifespan portal. Group narration computes once per round and reuses evidence; snapshots replay it without new retrieval.
**Tech Stack:** Python, anyio, FastAPI detached runs and existing snapshot machinery.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-roadmap-09-hybrid-historical-retrieval-design.md`, 09-S3 and sections 10–11.

## Global Constraints

- Privacy and module-scope acyclic imports apply; every test uses a temporary store and existing `llm_fakes.py`.
- `HISTORY_PHASE_SECONDS=4`; `HISTORY_PHASE_PLANNED_SECONDS=12` is the later planner seam. File stages can finish after the deadline; no network step may start after it.
- No retrieval call under a campaign lock. The synchronous bridge checks lock ownership in its calling worker before portal entry.
- Off/layout-off reads no historical documents and sends nothing. Exceptions degrade to empty/error evidence without stranding a posted player message; cancellation propagates.
- Group retrieval is narrator-only, once per round, with no appended continuation and no pending snapshot replay. Openers, mechanics continuations, replay and NPC contributions retrieve nothing.

## Review Focus

- A portal closing during shutdown must not strand the already appended player post.
- A lock held by the calling worker must be detected before crossing threads.
- Repeated narrator contributions in one round must share one evidence value.
- A stale outside-lock replay check must not override `_prepare`'s locked decision.
- A live inspector must send no embed/decide request.

### Task 1: Async retrieval seam and turn integration

scan text and private catalog weights from S1's preparation helper. It computes
enabled archive/full-recap omission ids from the same scan inputs as compose
and passes `summary_omissions=` into `retrieve`. Selection/expansion/refill
finish outside locks; no Query or expansion callback is passed into compose.
Test depth 1 with an archive-carried first candidate and a usable second one,
including the group narrator path, and assert no retrieval under its lock.

**Files:** Create `routes/history_recall.py`, `backend/tests/test_history_routes.py`, `backend/tests/test_history_lock_guard.py`; modify `store/history/retrieve.py`, `routes/scenes.py`, `routes/character_turns.py` and relevant imports/facades.

**Interfaces:** `async history.retrieve(cid, sid, query, *, ceiling, embed_client, deadline, rerank=None, origin="turn") -> Evidence`; `async gather(app, cid, sid, *, seed, resolved, llm_client, embed_client, deadline=None) -> Evidence`; `gather_sync(app, cid, sid, ...) -> Evidence`. S4 fills semantic through these seams. `_prepare`/`_compose` accept history; driver state retains it by round id. Dependency clients are passed through the turn setup without any extra construction on the off path.
