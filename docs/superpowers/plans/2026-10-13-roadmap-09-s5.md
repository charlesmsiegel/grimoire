# Roadmap 09-S5: optional captured history reranking

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Reorder admitted historical scenes with the existing relevance kit, softly and within the phase deadline.
**Architecture:** The route builds a single async reranker over 02's kit and the shared history-check route. It captures the decision scope, then records per-candidate grades/positions without combining signals or consulting a distribution.
**Tech Stack:** Existing decide operation, Score/Rank kit, decision capture and route resolution.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-roadmap-09-hybrid-historical-retrieval-design.md`, 09-S5 and section 7.3.

## Global Constraints

- Default `history_recall_rerank=off`; `RERANK_TOP=8`, `RERANK_CEILING=4` seconds, bounded by phase time remaining. Resolve in a worker through `_soft_resolved`/`require_inference("history-rerank", ..., operation="decide")`.
- Register `history_check` with Decision role and `routing.NO_LEGACY`, `history-rerank` in `NO_LEGACY_TASKS`, task policy no sampling/no escalation.
- Use `store.history_rerank`'s Score form by default; Rank form uses the kit's pointwise predicates and treats unplaced candidates as ungraded.
- Ungraded candidates keep their RRF positions; graded candidates reorder only among graded slots, ties retaining RRF order. Failure/refusal/abstention never vote out a candidate.
- Every call captures through `routes/decision_capture.py`; meter attribution includes campaign/scene/player post; never provider error text in records. Existing frozen inference baselines are not regenerated.

## Review Focus

- One unanswered candidate between graded candidates must keep its exact slot.
- A resolver failure or timeout must keep the previous order and expose the skip reason.
- Native Rank must carry pointwise wording and not depend on an abstain.
- Shared NO_LEGACY baseline exclusions must cover the new task without weakening old assertions.
- A prompt log scope must remain JSON-safe and preserve independent signal reasons.
