# Scene workspace

## Local reference intent (070)

The chat reference-intent panel stages a sketch or a typed description beside the existing composer. Import a PNG, JPEG or WebP of at most 2 MiB and 4096 × 4096 decoded pixels. No external image URL is fetched. Choose rectangle or ellipse, then drag across the sketch (mouse, touch or pen) to circle a spot; the labeled normalized x, y, width and height fields mirror the drag and remain the keyboard way to select a contained positive region. A tap, a drag smaller than 1% either way, or a cancelled pointer keeps the previous region. Typed intention and public region description work without an image.

The panel shows the **draft action** it would offer, for example "I hide here (Reference: doorway)". Nothing interprets the sketch and no model is sent it, so the table knows the spot only by the player's words: with a sketch, Use as draft asks for a public description of the circled spot before offering anything. Source/provenance, rights/permission and private notes/corrections stay with the local annotation and are never carried. **Use as draft** appends only the reviewed action and public region description to the current composer through its normal input event. It neither posts nor changes a map, actor, canonical record or evidence. Repeating an unchanged intent leaves the player’s edited composer alone. Cancel closes the panel without changing the composer. Clear reference discards the retained annotation and image without changing the composer.

### Carrying the marked sketch with the post

With an imported sketch, Use as draft also draws the region outline onto a copy of the sketch, at its own size and in its own image type, and puts that marked sketch in the composer's "Attached to your next post" tray. Posting then keeps it the way any picture attached to a post is kept ([MEDIA_SYNC_UI](MEDIA_SYNC_UI.md)): it becomes the campaign's own picture in the post's `post.<turn>` slot and is shown under the post. Its provenance origin is `player reference intent`, and its note gives the public description (cut to 1,500 characters), the shape and the normalized region and says it is not a world fact. The turn itself carries nothing of it, no character sees it, and no model is sent it; the imported, unmarked sketch is kept nowhere. Only the post's text reaches play.

A post carries one picture. An image the player attached themselves, or a made post's picture waiting for Try again, is never replaced: Use as draft refuses and asks them to remove it first. Changing the sketch, region or shape after attaching takes the old marked sketch off the next post, so it never goes out showing something the panel no longer does; Use as draft attaches the new one without touching the composer. Removing the reference's draft text from the composer also takes its sketch off, even when those words were already in the draft before Use as draft; editing around them does not. The marked sketch never goes without its text: taking the same intent again after its text was removed puts the text back with it. Clear reference takes the panel's marked sketch out of the tray; Remove in the tray leaves the draft text where it is. Coming back to the chat re-attaches the marked sketch while its draft text is still in the composer, and not otherwise. Drawing the marked sketch can take a moment, and circling waits for it; whatever the player typed or posted meanwhile is kept, and the draft text is appended to the composer as it then is. A post already on its way when the marked sketch is attached never takes it: the sketch and its words wait for the next post. If the region, shape or words change while it is drawn, nothing is added and the panel asks for Use as draft again. Once a post is made, its marked sketch belongs to that post: while it waits for Try again, Clear and edits leave it there, and the panel still reports when it goes. A chat that cannot keep pictures gets the text only, and the panel says so. If the marked sketch cannot be drawn or saved (no 2D canvas, an encoding the app does not accept, or over 32 MiB), neither the composer nor the tray changes. A post whose picture upload fails keeps the tray's existing Try again and Remove.

References are unsynced tab-memory drafts, scoped to the exact chat reference. They survive leaving and revisiting within the tab and disappear on reload. At most ten chats may retain annotations and one validated image per chat; clear an existing annotation to make room. No localStorage/sessionStorage image persistence occurs, and nothing is sent until the player posts. Preview object URLs are revoked on replacement, clear and page abort; revisiting recreates a preview from the retained file. Invalid import, invalid region and late cancelled decoding cannot replace a valid image or overwrite composer text. Import/decode failures and capacity errors appear in the panel’s live status.

This slice performs no image interpretation: the spot is the one the player circled and named. Inferred map positions/objects (milestone 2), gesture and live multimodal participation (milestone 3), carrying private provenance/rights/corrections, sync of unposted annotations and broader campaign workspace integration remain later owners.

## Authored VN presentation (072)

Choose VN presentation beside the transcript to read an authorized kept turn with its historical speaker names and verbatim text, including addressed/private/withheld post markers. The transcript DOM and composer stay owned by the chat page; switching presentation hides/shows the existing log without rewriting it or its scroll position. Previous turn, Next turn and the Kept turn chooser step through kept turns in transcript order.

**Cue timeline.** A cue belongs to one kept turn and records only what changes there. Blocking (left, centre, right or off stage, and which shown art) and the background carry forward to later turns until another cue changes them; focus, the transition and the note belong to the cue's own turn. Speakers nobody blocked are staged automatically in the first free place (centre, left, right), so two speakers share a place only when all three are taken, and keep that place on later turns. Focus defaults to the turn's last staged speaker; a cue may pick an actor or no focus. The Cue timeline lists every authored cue with a jump to its turn.

**Historical visibility.** Who may stand on the stage at a turn is the kept take's `present`, never the cast now. For a take written with no stage that is its whole cast. On a 041 stage it is only the player characters as of the take and the speakers of lines the table heard, each while physically on stage, so a hidden NPC the table never heard is not staged; a staged take that recorded no players stages no one. A carried or authored actor the table could not place at a turn is not drawn, and an authored reference to them is named under "Cue not honoured", so a later arrival is never staged early. On an unstaged take that means they had left: their blocking ends and the directions say so. On a 041 stage they may only be unheard, so their blocking is kept, undrawn, with no departure claimed, and they return to their authored place and art when the table next hears them. A silent present actor appears only when a cue blocks them. A cue may send an actor off stage; that carries forward too, and while off stage they are heard, not seen, until a later cue places them.

**Transitions and motion.** Transitions are none, fade (the frame and arriving actors fade in) and move (each actor whose place changed slides from its previous column). They play only when the reader arrives at a turn, not on every redraw. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns every animation off. Each turn also lists plain-text stage directions (entrances, moves, departures, outfit changes, focus, background), so no change depends on motion or colour. One persistent polite live region announces them when the reader arrives at a turn, not on redraws. Focus is marked by an outline, a dimmer stage for the others and the caption, never colour alone.

**Art.** A cue names a slot: an outfit's expression sprite (`expr.<outfit>.<name>`), else the portrait or avatar, taken from the actor's current shown art. Hidden and unavailable slots are never offered or requested. With no matching art an initial stands in. Current authorized art is decorative and makes no historical wardrobe claim; the cue's choice of outfit is the authored record. Removed cue turns, actors, selected art and backgrounds are marked unavailable rather than rebound.

**Storage.** Cues are device-local, unsynced view metadata in versioned localStorage, scoped to the exact chat and kept turn: at most 100 cues per chat and 1000 characters per note, holding only actor ids, places, slots, an existing backdrop's image ref and the note, never transcript text. Every tab of the device shares the store, and each kept turn's cue is its own stored entry: Save cue and Reset cue write or remove only that turn's entry, so two tabs editing different turns keep both even when their saves interleave, and other tabs take the change up. #694's per-tab (sessionStorage) cues are carried into the device store once, never over a turn the device already stores (readable or not), resuming on the next load if storage fills partway, and still stage their own turn only, as they did. Cues saved by a newer version are kept and reported rather than overwritten; malformed data and unavailable/quota storage produce a visible warning; failed writes retain an explicitly unsaved in-memory value that survives other tabs' changes and is written with the next successful save. Reset local cues removes every cue of the chat on this device, unreadable ones included, and is the recovery control for them. No canonical write, provider call, media write or remote image URL input is introduced.

Historical art snapshots, cue synchronization/revision integration, line-specific cues, inferred or rule-based cue suggestions and adaptive cinematic composition (milestone 3) remain future work, as does broader 115 integration.
