# Rooftop

Officially locked since a third-year incident a decade ago. Unofficially open to anyone who knows about the loose tile to the right of the door, where the spare key has been replaced and re-hidden every year by a different graduating class. The teachers know. The teachers do not say.

The view is of the courtyard cherry tree from above, the baseball ground, and the rooftops of Sakuradai stretching toward the train tracks. In summer, the metal of the door handle is too hot to touch in the afternoon. In winter, you can see your breath. Cicadas in August are deafening up here. Wind in October moves your hair the way only a rooftop wind does.

This is where the cast comes to be alone, or to be alone together. Kaito Asano eats lunch up here most days, sketchbook open. Souta Kirishima sleeps up here when he hasn't slept the night before. The roof is where the difficult conversations happen — the ones that need sky.
