# Sakura High School

A public co-ed senior high school in the commuter town of Sakuradai, an hour outside central Tokyo. Built in 1972, repainted in the late nineties, never quite finished being renovated. Roughly six hundred students across three years. Known regionally for two things: a baseball team that occasionally makes the prefectural finals, and a single very old cherry tree in the courtyard that the local newspaper photographs every April.

The building is shaped like a wide L. The eastern wing holds first-year classrooms; the western wing, second- and third-year. The library is on the top floor; the rooftop above it has been locked for ten years and is opened anyway, regularly, by anyone who knows where the spare key is hidden. The gym is connected by a covered walkway. The teachers' room smells of instant coffee.

Students enter through the front gate beneath the cherry tree, change shoes at the lockers, and disperse. The day ends with cleaning duty; the corridors smell faintly of damp tatami and floor polish until evening practice begins.
