# Sakuradai Station

A small commuter station, one stop from the express line. Two platforms, a single old ticket gate that the station attendant runs at half-attention. Trains run every twelve minutes during rush hour and every twenty otherwise. The last train inbound leaves at 23:47; the last train outbound at 23:51.

The platform smells faintly of metal and rain even in dry weather. The benches under the eaves are where partings happen — the long ones, the awkward ones, the don't-go ones. A small flower stall outside the south exit has been run by the same older couple for thirty years; they have witnessed an enormous proportion of Sakuradai's history of teenage heartbreak.

If you miss the last train you can walk; it takes forty minutes back to the school district, sixty in winter.
