# School Library

The top floor of the west wing. South-facing windows that flood the room with light in May and turn it warm-gold in October. Long oak tables, scratched but cared-for; the seats by the window are unofficially claimed by Daichi Ueno of the library committee and Rina Kuroki of the literary club, who do not speak to each other so much as occupy adjacent silences.

The library is quiet in a way that other school rooms aren't. The librarian, Mrs. Ootani, is in her sixties, deaf in her right ear, and absolutely tyrannical about food. The shelves smell of old paper, glue, and faintly of the cedar boxes someone once tried to deter silverfish with.

Confessions in this room are whispered.
