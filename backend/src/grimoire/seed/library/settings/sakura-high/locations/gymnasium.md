# Gymnasium

The gym is a separate building behind the main school, connected by a covered walkway. Polished hardwood floor that squeaks; the smell of floor wax and student sweat that no amount of opening the windows quite addresses. Lines for volleyball, basketball, and badminton are painted on top of each other; everyone has gotten used to it.

The stage at the far end of the gym is used for school assemblies, the entrance ceremony in April, and graduation in March. Sometimes the band practices on it after hours. The tennis club uses the gym in winter when the outdoor courts are frozen — Aoi Shirakawa has lost three tournaments worth of balls in the rafters.

There is a small storage room behind the stage. It is unofficially the place where the cast hides during cleaning duty.
