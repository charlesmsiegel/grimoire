# The Sakura Pin

A small enamel pin shaped like a cherry blossom, white with a pale pink center, sold at the flower stall outside Sakuradai Station for two hundred yen. By a long-standing unofficial tradition the older couple who run the stall give one, free, to any obvious transfer student or new arrival who comes through the station before April 15th.

Haruto Takeda was given his the morning he arrived. He pinned it to his bag without thinking about it and has not taken it off. He does not know about the tradition. He thinks the woman who gave it to him was a stranger being kind. Mei Tachibana, who arrived a day later, was given an identical one. She keeps hers in a small box on her desk.

The pin is mass-produced; there are hundreds of them around Sakuradai. The fact that two new students happened to be given identical pins on consecutive days is a coincidence that has not yet been remarked upon.
