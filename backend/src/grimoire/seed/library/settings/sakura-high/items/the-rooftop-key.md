# The Rooftop Key

A small brass key, dulled with age, that opens the metal door from the east stairwell onto the school rooftop. The official key is on the deputy headmaster's keyring; this is the spare, which has lived behind the loose tile to the right of the door, replaced every spring by a different outgoing third-year.

The teachers know. The deputy headmaster has known for at least eight years. The arrangement persists because the rooftop is, in practice, the only place at Sakura High where a student can be entirely alone — or entirely alone with someone — and the teachers have decided, in their wisdom, that they would rather know where the trouble happens than not.

The current spare was placed there by a girl named Inoue who graduated last spring. She left a small folded note under the tile with the key, which read: *please return it before you leave*. Two of the current third-years have read the note. Kaito Asano is one of them.
