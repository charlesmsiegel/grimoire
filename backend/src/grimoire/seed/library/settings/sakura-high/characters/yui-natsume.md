# Yui Natsume

## Appearance
Average height, restless hands. Dark brown hair cut sharp at the chin; bangs she trims herself, badly, every few weeks. Eyes that narrow when she's amused. Ink-stained fingertips year-round. She wears her sleeves pushed up; the cuffs are always smudged. A small enamel pin of a tanuki on her bag.

## Personality
Defensive, funny, sharper than she means to be. She loves her friends ferociously and would die before saying so. Her first reaction to feelings is to insult them. She is talented — really talented; she's been drawing manga since she was eleven and submitting to magazines since she was fourteen — and she expects herself to be better than she is. She is much, much softer than the front suggests.

## Background
Middle child of three; older sister is a model, younger brother is a prodigy violinist. Yui has been the funny one by default, which she resents and leans into. She co-runs the art club with Kaito Asano; they have an old, comfortable rivalry. She submitted a one-shot to a major magazine last year. It was rejected. She has not told anyone.

## What she wants
To be taken seriously without losing the version of herself she likes — the loud, the sharp, the funny. Someone who can match her insults without flinching, and call her on the deflection when it matters. She would die before admitting any of this.

## Romance notes
The tsundere, classic. The romance with Yui is built around the cracks — catching her drawing you, catching her crying about a rejection slip, getting one straight sincere sentence out of her by accident. She will not confess. She will hand you a manuscript with a character in it that looks suspiciously like you. The character will be loved by another character who looks suspiciously like her.
