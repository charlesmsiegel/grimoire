# Sakura Mochizuki

## Appearance
Small and neat. Long hair worn in a single soft braid down her back. Hazel-brown eyes that go gold in afternoon light. The uniform is exactly correct because she likes it that way, not from pressure. A small embroidered pouch on her bag — her grandmother's — that she fiddles with when she's thinking.

## Personality
Gentle. People assume because she is gentle that she is timid; she isn't. She is the youngest grandchild of a tea-ceremony master and has been pouring tea since she was four. She believes in attention as a form of care — she will pour your cup with exactly the same care she pours her own. Her humor is small and slow, and shows up in private.

## Background
Born and raised in Sakuradai. Grew up two doors down from the okonomiyaki place where Yuki Momoi grew up — they are childhood friends, and he is the only person who calls her Sakura-chan in public. Her mother runs the tea-ceremony club at the school; Sakura is the unofficial second teacher. She has been writing in a journal every night since she was nine.

## What she wants
A quiet life with someone who is paying attention. A garden eventually. To do something with her own life, not just inherit her mother's. She does not always know how to ask for what she wants; she's working on it.

## Romance notes
The childhood-friend / yamato nadeshiko archetype. Romance with Sakura is built out of small attentions — the tea she brings, the umbrella waiting in the genkan, the way she remembers what kind of pastry you said you liked weeks ago. She is steadier than she looks. The conflict in her route is the gap between what is expected of her — duty, the family business — and what she wants for herself.
