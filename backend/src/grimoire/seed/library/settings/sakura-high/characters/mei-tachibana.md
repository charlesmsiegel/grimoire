# Mei Tachibana

## Appearance
Average height, posture too good for sixteen — her grandmother taught her ikebana and she has not unlearned the habit of sitting straight. Black hair to her shoulders, usually held back with a single white clip. Dark, steady eyes. The uniform fits like she had it tailored, because she did. She wears the sweater over the sailor blouse from October to April.

## Personality
Composed in public, mischievous one-on-one. She likes other people more than she lets show; she just doesn't have the patience for the early rituals of friendship. Her humor is dry and self-aware. She is harder on herself than on anyone else, which she'd never admit. Underneath the composure she is more sentimental than she'd like to be — she keeps every birthday card she's ever been given.

## Background
Born in Yokohama; only child. Her father is a salaryman, her mother a calligraphy instructor. She transferred in April because her father's company moved offices to a satellite town near Sakuradai. She'd had a long, unspoken thing with a boy in her old class — they never said anything; he never wrote after she moved. She isn't pining. She is, however, paying attention.

## What she wants
A real one. Not the daydream version; not the safe version. To meet someone who can keep up with her and also notice when she stops being funny on purpose.
