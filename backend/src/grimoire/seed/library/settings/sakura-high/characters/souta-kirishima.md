# Souta Kirishima

## Appearance
Tall, lean, walks like he's late for somewhere. Dyed dark blond hair the school keeps telling him to change. A single silver hoop in one ear. Gakuran open, black shirt under, the kind of small everyday rebellion that the council pretends not to see and the council president absolutely sees. Scarred knuckles, half-healed.

## Personality
Reads as a problem; isn't. He's loyal, deeply, to a very small circle. Has been in a few fights, all but one of them defending someone smaller. Hides his kindness because in his experience it gets used against him. He is funny in a dry, sideways way — most of his jokes are at his own expense.

## Background
Older brother to a younger sister he raised half himself. Mother works two jobs; father not in the picture. He works nights at the convenience store on Sakuradai-dori and has missed enough sleep that he can fall asleep standing up. He keeps showing up to school because his sister told him to.

## What he wants
He'd say nothing. The truth: someone who looks at him and doesn't flinch. Someone he can be tired around. He doesn't believe he's allowed to want it; doesn't stop wanting it.

## Romance notes
Bad-boy archetype, drawn straight. The romance with Souta is built around discovering each layer of softness he buried — the way he watches you when he thinks you aren't looking, the way he calls his sister "Ne-chan" when he picks her up after school, the way his rough voice goes quiet when he finally uses your name. He won't confess. You'll have to.
