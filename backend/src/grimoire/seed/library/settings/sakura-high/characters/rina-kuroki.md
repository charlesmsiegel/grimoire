# Rina Kuroki

## Appearance
Slight, slim, pale; long dark hair that she does not put up. A small silver cross at her collar that the school has decided not to make a thing of. The uniform is correct but somehow rearranged — a black ribbon where the regulation white one should be, the cardigan in November even when it isn't cold. She reads while she walks.

## Personality
Quiet. Considered. She is not unfriendly, but she does not perform friendliness; people often think she is colder than she is. Her humor is dry, literary, and deeply self-aware — once you notice her jokes you cannot stop noticing them. She is sentimental about books, music in minor keys, and weather. She is more lonely than she would ever admit.

## Background
Daughter of a translator and a violinist; spent two years of childhood in Vienna and writes about it sometimes. She runs the literary club, which has four members. She is rumored to write poetry under a pen name in a small online journal; the rumor is correct. She is friends, in her way, with Kaito Asano — they sit at opposite ends of the rooftop in companionable silence.

## What she wants
A reader. Someone who can see what she means without her saying it. Someone she can be silly with — a thing almost no one believes she could be. She thinks she is unromanceable. She is not.

## Romance notes
The kuudere. The romance with Rina is built out of subtext: the line she underlined in the book she lent you, the umbrella she had ready, the day she finally laughs in front of you. She will not confess in words. She will say it in a poem, or by a flower in your locker, or by sitting beside you in a thunderstorm without saying anything at all.
