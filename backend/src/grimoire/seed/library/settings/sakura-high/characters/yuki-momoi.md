# Yuki Momoi

## Appearance
Shorter than Ren but built like the catcher he is — broad shoulders, the tan you get from practicing on a Sakuradai field all summer. Short black hair he doesn't comb. A constellation of tiny scars on his hands from foul tips. He smiles with his whole face.

## Personality
Sunshine. Genuinely. He likes people and assumes people like him, and is so reliably himself that the assumption holds. He is not stupid — his grades are middling because he doesn't care, not because he can't. He cares deeply about the team, about his sister, and about whoever he is currently in love with, which he will tell you, loudly, in the lunchroom.

## Background
Local kid, eldest brother of two. His parents run a small okonomiyaki place near the station; he works the grill weekends. Started baseball at six because his uncle coached. Captains the team this year by virtue of being the loudest person on it.

## What he wants
Right now: to win the prefectural qualifiers. Always: to make the people around him happy. He doesn't have hidden depths so much as obvious ones he doesn't bother to disguise.

## Romance notes
Forward. Cheerful and a little oblivious. If he likes you he will tell you within a week and ask you to a tournament. The complication isn't whether he means it — he does — it's whether you are ready to be loved that openly, by someone the whole class will know about by Tuesday.
