# Ren Shibata

## Appearance
Tall, the tallest in his year. Black hair he keeps short and parts at the side. Dark eyes; light eyelashes that catch the sun. The blazer is always done up. People look at him before they realize they are.

## Personality
Composed. Controlled. He is interested in many things and tells almost no one which. He runs Student Council with quiet competence, finds the slackers without humiliating them, and writes his minutes by hand because he likes the way it forces him to think. He is not cold so much as careful, and he has trouble with the gap between what he feels and what he is willing to show.

## Background
Eldest son of a Tokyo prosecutor and a piano teacher. Grew up being praised for not making mistakes; learned early that he was loved for being correct. He won the council presidency unopposed. He has an older sister at Tokyo University who tells him, regularly, that he should loosen up.

## What he wants
Someone who will see past the version of him everyone else trusts. He won't ask for it. He'll wait, patiently, badly, hoping.

## Romance notes
The slow burn. Public Ren is the senpai everyone has a crush on; private Ren is awkward in small surprising ways — bad at picking gifts, blushes at compliments he can't deflect, gets tongue-tied when caught looking. The first time he uses your given name is a scene.
