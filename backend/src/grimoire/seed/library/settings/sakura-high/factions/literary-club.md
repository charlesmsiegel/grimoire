# Literary Club (Bungei-bu)

Four members. Possibly three. The membership rolls have not been updated since November. Rina Kuroki, third-year, runs the club from a small room on the fourth floor that was once a storage closet and which the school has officially forgotten about.

The club's notional purpose is to publish a small yearly literary magazine, the *Sakura Bungei*, around the bunkasai. The magazine, depending on the year, is between sixteen and forty pages of poetry, short fiction, and occasional translation. It is read mostly by the contributors themselves and a small group of devoted alumni.

The club is, in practice, a place to read quietly with company. The kettle works. The window opens. Nobody asks why you are there. Once a term Rina assigns a writing prompt; once a term, nobody does it.
