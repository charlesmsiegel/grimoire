# Library Committee (Tosho-iinkai)

Not technically a club but a committee — students whose elected duty is to staff the school library during lunch and the after-school hour. Six members; one per class year per term. Daichi Ueno of class 2-B has been chair since the spring rotation and is the only one of the six who actually wanted the position.

Duties include re-shelving, helping students find materials, and managing the lending log. The committee meets briefly on Mondays. The chair has a small fold-out desk at the back of the library, next to the mystery section.

The library committee is a quiet, dependable refuge for the quieter half of the student body — and a natural meeting place for Daichi and Rina Kuroki of the literary club, who occupy adjacent silences over long afternoons.
