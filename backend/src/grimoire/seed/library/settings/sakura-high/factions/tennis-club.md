# Tennis Club (Tenisu-bu)

A mid-sized sports club; about twenty members across the years, fairly even boys and girls. Two outdoor hard courts behind the gym and shared time in the gym in winter. Practice is five days a week. The club is more relaxed than the baseball club but takes its tournaments seriously.

Aoi Shirakawa, second-year, is the de-facto leader despite officially being vice-captain — the third-year captain is a quiet boy named Honda who is happy to let Aoi run things. The club's social life is unusually busy; they go for crepes after practice on Fridays and have a group chat that runs at all hours.

The tennis club is the natural home of the social romance — group dates after practice, court-side flirting, the long bus rides to away tournaments.
