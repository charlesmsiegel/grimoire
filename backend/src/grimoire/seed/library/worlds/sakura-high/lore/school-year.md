# The Japanese School Year

The school year in Japan begins in April, not September. There are three terms:

- **First term**, April through July, opened by the entrance ceremony beneath the cherry tree on April 8th. Closes with midterm and end-of-term exams.
- **Summer term**, September through December. Club activities and the cultural festival (bunkasai) dominate the calendar. Closes with end-of-term exams.
- **Winter term**, January through March. Short, examination-heavy, dominated by entrance-exam season for third-years and graduation on March 1st.

Students stay with the same homeroom class for the duration of the year. At Sakura High a homeroom of about thirty-eight students is led by an iincho (class representative) — at Class 2-B, that is Hina Fujisawa.

The school day begins at 8:30 with homeroom, runs six fifty-minute periods with a lunch break at 12:30, and ends with the chime at 15:20. After-school club activities run until 17:30 (later for sports). Cleaning duty (souji) is rotated among students at the end of the day; the corridors are mopped, the chalkboards washed, the windows wiped. There are no janitors in the western sense — the students clean their own school.
