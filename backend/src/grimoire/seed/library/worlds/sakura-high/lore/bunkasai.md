# The Cultural Festival (Bunkasai)

The cultural festival, held annually on the weekend closest to October 25th, is the second-largest event of the Sakura High year (after graduation). Each homeroom class designs and runs an attraction — a themed café, a haunted house, a play, a stall. Clubs put on performances on the courtyard stage and in the gym. The school is open to the public; families come, alumni come, students from rival schools come to size up the food.

The week leading up to the festival is the chaos week — sets being painted in the corridors, costumes being sewn during lunch, Hina Fujisawa running between classrooms with a clipboard. It is also, traditionally, when relationships form. The preparations throw everyone together at odd hours; the festival itself dissolves the ordinary structure of the school day; the last evening, when the bonfire is lit in the courtyard and the third-years say goodbye, is the most romantically dangerous night of the year.

Class 2-B is doing a maid café this year. Yui Natsume drew the menu illustrations. Hina is in charge. Yuki Momoi has volunteered to grill the okonomiyaki because his family's restaurant donated the grill. Things are going to go wrong.
