# Kaito Asano

## Appearance
Slim, a little pale, the kind of face that looks tired without being unhealthy. Black hair grown out enough to fall over one eye. Headphones permanently around his neck even in class; he gets in trouble for it and does it anyway. Long fingers. Faint pencil smudge on the side of his left hand from sketching.

## Personality
Reserved, but not aloof — once you know him he is gentle, observant, fiercely loyal to the very few people he lets in. He hates pretense; he hates being asked to perform. He plays guitar well enough that the art club has asked him to play at the cultural festival three years running and he has refused twice.

## Background
Only child of divorced parents; lives with his mother above her secondhand bookshop on the shopping street. His father is a session musician in Osaka who calls on his birthday and forgets the rest of the year. Kaito has been drawing since he could hold a pencil and writing songs since he stopped speaking to his father.

## What he wants
To finish a song without throwing it away. To be seen by someone who doesn't ask him to be anything else. He doesn't expect either; he is, however, hopeful.

## Romance notes
The slow, quiet kind. He notices everything about you long before he says anything. The first real intimacy is hearing him hum, or being shown a page of his sketchbook. The first time he uses your name in a song is the confession. He may not realize that's what it is.
