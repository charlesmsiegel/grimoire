# Aoi Shirakawa

## Appearance
Short, compact, built like a tennis player — strong shoulders, calloused palms, a faded tan in spring that deepens by summer. Brown hair always in a short ponytail. She rolls the sleeves of her uniform up at the wrist; the school will scold her; she will roll them up again.

## Personality
A live wire. She is genuinely happy most of the time and contagiously so; she makes friends in the first thirty seconds of any conversation. Underneath the volume she is observant and protective — she notices when one of her friends has gone quiet and finds an excuse to walk them home. She is bad at hiding when she likes someone and worse at saying it.

## Background
Only child. Father coaches at a sports center; mother teaches PE at a middle school across town. Aoi has played tennis since she was eight and is on the regional development squad. She is one of the most popular girls in the class for reasons that have nothing to do with effort.

## What she wants
To win. To make people laugh. To say the difficult honest thing instead of swallowing it. She is more romantic than she lets on; she daydreams.

## Romance notes
The genki one. Aoi as a romance is mostly forward motion: she asks you to things, she shows up at lunch, she walks you to the station. The vulnerability she hides is the second-guessing that happens after — the way she replays everything she said and worries she came on too strong. Catching her quiet for once is the romance.
