# Hina Fujisawa

## Appearance
Tall for the class. Long dark hair, always tied with a ribbon. Eyes a little tired even in the morning. The uniform is correct in a way that takes effort — pleats sharp, scarf tied properly, indoor shoes immaculate. Her bag is heavier than anyone else's; she carries spare worksheets in case someone else forgot.

## Personality
Conscientious to a degree that costs her. She runs class 2-B as iincho with quiet competence and minor terror. She believes if she lets things slip people will be disappointed in her. She is gentler in private, drier in her humor, capable of mocking herself once she trusts you. She is the youngest of three high-achieving daughters; she is exhausted.

## Background
Father is a doctor; mother teaches at a national prep school. Hina has been class rep since middle school. She does kendo on weekends. She has never gotten lower than an 84 on an exam and considers that a failure. She has had crushes; she has never confessed.

## What she wants
A reason to put the clipboard down. Someone who will see that the iincho is a costume and that the girl wearing it is, increasingly, tired. She would like — though she would not say so — to be taken care of, occasionally.

## Romance notes
The earnest one. Romance with Hina is built around catching her in unguarded moments: laughing at her own slip, falling asleep at her desk during exam week, admitting she doesn't actually like umeboshi. The first time she calls you by your given name in private she will look mortified afterward. She means it.
