# Haruto Takeda

## Appearance
A little taller than average, with the kind of build that says he ran track in middle school and stopped. Dark hair he forgets to cut. Brown eyes that go a shade darker when he's interested in something. The uniform fits well enough; the top button is always undone. There's an old white pin on his bag — a sakura blossom in enamel, given to him at the train station the day he moved.

## Personality
Polite to a fault until someone needles him; then a dry, observant sense of humor surfaces. He listens. He's not shy so much as careful — he wants to understand a room before stepping into it. Around people he trusts, he laughs easily, makes self-deprecating jokes, and slides into Kansai cadence. He underestimates himself.

## Background
Born and raised in Osaka. His mother took a transfer to a Tokyo office in March; the family followed. He left a girl he had not quite asked out and a baseball team he'd half-quit, neither of them properly resolved. He arrived at Sakura High the second week of April — a week late, in a borrowed uniform, knowing no one.

## What he wants
Not to be the new kid forever. To find his footing. Somewhere underneath: to be known by someone for who he actually is, rather than as the polite transfer student from Osaka.
