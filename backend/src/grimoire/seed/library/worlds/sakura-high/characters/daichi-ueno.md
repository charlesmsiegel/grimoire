# Daichi Ueno

## Appearance
Average height, slight build, the kind of face that reads younger than it is. Glasses that slide down his nose. Hair he combs but a cowlick that does not consent. The uniform is always exactly correct. Hands inkstained at the fingertips during exam season.

## Personality
A quiet, careful kindness. He is observant — he notices when someone hasn't eaten lunch, when someone needs an excuse to leave a conversation. He volunteers for things no one else wants. His humor is dry and slow and very, very gentle; if you catch one of his jokes you feel like you've been let into a club.

## Background
Middle son of three. Father is a city librarian; mother teaches calligraphy in the same room your mother used to. He has loved books since he could read them, and is currently reading through the school library's mystery section in publication order out of stubborn completionism.

## What he wants
To matter to someone the way the heroines of his favorite novels matter to the people who narrate them. He doesn't say this out loud. He's not sure he is allowed to want it.

## Romance notes
The childhood-friend feeling, except you've only just met. Steady. The romance with Daichi is built out of small things — saving each other seats, walking home, the slow shift from -san to -kun to first name. If you confess, he will cry, very quietly, and then say thank you.
