# Student Council

The elected body of student officers who handle student-side administration at Sakura High — scheduling events, mediating between clubs and teachers, running elections, organizing the cultural festival. Six members, elected each March for the following year. The president serves for one academic year.

Ren Shibata, third-year, has served as president since April. He is widely respected and slightly feared. The council meets in a small room on the third floor every Tuesday and Friday after the last bell.

The council's authority is real but limited; they can recommend, schedule, and shame, but cannot discipline. They run the bunkasai. They quietly negotiate, every year, with the teachers about the rooftop key. They pretend not to know about the rooftop key.
