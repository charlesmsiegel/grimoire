# Tea Ceremony Club (Sadou-bu)

A small club, eight members, that meets in the school's tatami room on the first floor — a single eight-mat room reserved for tea ceremony and the occasional flower-arrangement lesson. The faculty advisor is Mochizuki-sensei, who runs the local tea school and is Sakura Mochizuki's mother.

Sakura is technically vice-captain but in practice leads sessions; her mother trusts her enough to leave the room for the second half of most meetings. The club practices the omotesenke style of chanoyu. They host a small open ceremony at the cultural festival each year, served in the tatami room, three guests at a time.

The club is the natural home of the quiet romance — the slow unhurried attention of the form, the practiced grace, the bow before and after. A scene in the tea room moves at the speed of the kettle.
