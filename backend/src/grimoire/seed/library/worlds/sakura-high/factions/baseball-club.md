# Baseball Club (Yakyu-bu)

The most-attended sports club at Sakura High; thirty-some members across the three years. Practice is six days a week — five afternoons after school and Saturday morning. The club has made the prefectural quarter-finals twice in the last decade; this year, with Yuki Momoi captaining, they are hopeful.

The coach is Hayashi-sensei, who teaches geography and considers baseball his real job. Practices are loud, the catcalls between the players are constant, and the post-practice trip to the okonomiyaki place is a club institution.

The club has historically been Sakura High's most public face. The class often turns out to watch summer tournaments. A romance with Yuki involves, eventually, being recognized in the stands.
