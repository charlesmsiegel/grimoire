# Art Club (Bijutsu-bu)

A small, scrappy, well-loved club. About twelve members across the three years, co-run by Kaito Asano (third-year, painting and music) and Yui Natsume (second-year, manga and illustration), who alternately defer to each other and fight about everything. The clubroom is on the fourth floor, the smallest in the corner, smelling permanently of turpentine and instant coffee.

The club's annual responsibility at the cultural festival is the entrance mural — a different theme each year, painted on the big plywood panel that hangs above the front gate. This year's theme is uncharacteristically a joint project between Kaito (background) and Yui (figures); they have not yet agreed on the figures.

The art club is the natural home of the romance-adjacent introvert. It is where Kaito hides, where Yui processes, and where, surprisingly often, students from other clubs come to draw quietly when their own club practice is too much.
