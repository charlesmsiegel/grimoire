# Transfer Student Arrival

It's the second Tuesday of April. The cherry tree out front has dropped most of its petals; they're still gathered in the corners of the courtyard, banked up against the planters like pink snow. The chime sounds at 8:30. Homeroom begins. You — the transfer — wait outside in the corridor with Kobayashi-sensei, who is rummaging in her folder for the right form and apologizing about the holdup.

"You'll be fine," she says, not looking up. "2-B is a good class. Loud. Iincho will look after you."

She pushes the door open. There is the scrape of chairs as the class stands, and the bow, and the smell of chalk dust and floor wax and someone's mother's perfume drifting off a uniform two rows back. Thirty-eight faces. The class rep — tall, ribbon in her hair — bows precisely and gestures to the empty seat by the window. The boy in the row behind it leans forward immediately and stage-whispers something that gets him hushed by the girl across the aisle.

"Class, this is your new classmate. They've come to us from —" Kobayashi-sensei glances at her form. "Please introduce yourself."

A breeze comes in through the open windows. The petals in the courtyard lift, briefly, and settle. Everyone is waiting.
