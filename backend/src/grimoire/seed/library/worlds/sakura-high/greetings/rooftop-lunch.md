# Rooftop Lunch

A month into the term. You've worked out that the rooftop door isn't really locked, and that the loose tile to its right is loose for a reason. Lunch period is twenty-three minutes old. You've climbed the east stairwell, pushed the door open, and stepped out into a wide, hot square of sky.

The rooftop is empty except for one person.

Kaito Asano — third-year, art club, the boy with the headphones — is sitting on the far corner with his back against the chainlink, sketchbook on his knees, a melon bread half-eaten beside him. He has not noticed you yet, or is pretending not to have. He is humming, very softly, a melody you don't quite recognize and might not, once you've heard it again, be able to forget.

The door clicks shut behind you. He looks up.
