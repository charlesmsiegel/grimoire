# The Cherry Tree Courtyard

The first thing you see when you come through the front gate. A single ancient cherry tree, dark-trunked, gnarled, broader than the courtyard tiles can quite hold. In late March and early April it makes the entire front of the school pink. The local newspaper photographs it every year. Couples photograph it more often.

There is a stone bench facing it. The bench is famous for two things: a small carved heart in the back left corner whose initials nobody recognizes anymore, and the school superstition that a confession made beneath the tree on the day the first blossom opens cannot be refused.

The superstition is, of course, nonsense. It is also extensively documented in the school yearbook — every spring, someone tries.
