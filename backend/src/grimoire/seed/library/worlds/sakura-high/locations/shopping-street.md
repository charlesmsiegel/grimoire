# Sakuradai-dori

The covered shopping street that runs from the train station to the river. Half the shops are older than the high school. The arcade roof is corrugated plastic that turns the light yellow on sunny days and drums in the rain. The smell shifts as you walk it — coffee, then okonomiyaki, then old paper, then the metal-and-cigarette smell of the pachinko parlor at the south end.

The okonomiyaki place is run by Yuki Momoi's family; he grills weekends. The little bookshop above which Kaito Asano lives is two doors down. There's a florist who knows the names of half the school by face. The shoutengai is where the cast bumps into each other on the way home, where umbrellas are shared in unexpected rain, where small confrontations happen that the school never witnesses.
