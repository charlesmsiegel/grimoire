# Café Petit Rose

A small café halfway down the shopping street, narrow and warm and run by an older woman named Watanabe-san who used to be a flight attendant on the Paris route and never forgave the company that retired her. The walls are covered in French film posters; the menu is mostly espresso drinks, one kind of toast, and a slice of strawberry cheesecake.

It is the unofficial after-school study spot for the second-years of class 2-B. Hina Fujisawa drinks black coffee here and pretends to like it. Daichi Ueno reads. Mei Tachibana, if she's free, takes the corner table by the window. The owner refers to her regulars by family name and has been known to refuse to serve a boy who was rude to a girl on a previous visit.

In autumn the windows steam over. In winter the heater above the door makes a small clicking sound every four minutes. It is, by careful design, romantic.
