# Class 2-B Classroom

A second-year classroom on the second floor of the west wing. Tall windows along one wall give onto the courtyard and the cherry tree; in April the room is half-pink with reflected blossom. Thirty-eight desks, the chalkboard at the front, the teacher's lectern slightly off-center where someone shoved it years ago. The class poster is hand-lettered and slightly crooked.

Class 2-B is Hina Fujisawa's class to run as iincho, and she runs it well. The homeroom teacher is Kobayashi-sensei — modern Japanese — who is fond of the class in a tired, affectionate way and who arrives three minutes late to every morning meeting. The class has a reputation among teachers as "the noisy one"; among students as "the fun one."

Most of the cast spends most of their day here. The seat by the window in the third row is where the transfer student sits — the iconic seat, with the breeze and the view of the courtyard.
