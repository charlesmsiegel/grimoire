Judge 1

Theory: An evaluation is an explicit behavioral hypothesis attached to a synthetic case, production prompt/consumer paths and checks whose counterexamples are replayable. Live runs add measured model behavior and cost provenance, while offline replay proves that the harness detects declared violations.

Instead of: Instead of interpreting replay success as model quality or missing cost as zero, the harness separates conformance from live evidence, preserves partial/error states and stores priced results for comparison without reopening a library.
