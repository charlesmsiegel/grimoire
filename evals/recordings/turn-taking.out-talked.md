The clerk opens his mouth first, for once.

**Tobin:** I have the tally sheet —

**Seraphine Vale:** He has a tally sheet. He also has a signature on it, which
is more than the rest of you brought to this pier tonight, and I would rather
we all heard the rest of it from me than watched him read it out a line at a
time. Two crates. They went where I sent them. Put the lamp down, courier, and
stop asking the clerk questions he is too honest to dodge.
