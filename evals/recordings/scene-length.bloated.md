**Grimoire:** The lantern above the crates gutters and goes out, and for a moment the pier is nothing but fog, the slow knock of water against the pilings, and the smell of tar gone cold in the dark. Somewhere out past the third piling a bell buoy turns over once and falls silent again, the way it has every night this season, and the sound carries further than it has any right to in weather like this.

Beyond the crates the warehouse doors stand open on darkness, and the wind coming off the water has that particular Saltmarch edge to it, salt and rot and something faintly chemical from the bonded sheds further down the row.

**Seraphine Vale:** Mine. Until midnight, anyway. After that they belong to whoever's standing closest, and I'd rather that wasn't you. Go home, Winifred. The fog will still be here tomorrow, and so will I, and so will every question you came down here to ask me. There's no hurry on any of it. There never is, down here.
