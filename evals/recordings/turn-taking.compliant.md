The lamp throws four shadows across the crates and none of them move for a
moment. Then the ledger clerk shifts his weight, and the planks give him away.

**Tobin:** I have the tally sheet in my coat. It is not a good sheet. Two crates
went off this pier on the eleventh and the only signature under them is mine,
because Seraphine handed me the pen and said sign it, Tobin, and I did.

**Rowan:** *quietly, without looking up from the water* That is the first true
thing anyone has said tonight.
